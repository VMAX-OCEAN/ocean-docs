# Scientific Analytics

## Residual
E(x,y,z,t) = O(x,y,z,t) − M(x,y,z,t)

Research co-location, interpolation, quality control and uncertainty.

## Anomaly
Compare against climatology, baseline or ensemble mean.

## Uncertainty
Model + observation + interpolation + temporal mismatch + spatial mismatch.

## Feature extraction
Eddies, fronts, upwelling, thermocline, chlorophyll blooms.
Candidate methods: gradients, vorticity, Okubo-Weiss, contours, connected components.

## Trajectory analysis
For x(t), y(t), z(t), sample M(x(t),y(t),z(t),t) and compare with O(t).

## Temporal tracking
Represent feature state as position, size, intensity, velocity and uncertainty.

## AI policy
Use AI only when it adds measurable value: feature classification, query assistance or forecasting. Do not add AI as decoration.

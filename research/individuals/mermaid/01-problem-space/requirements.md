# Problem Space

## Data
- Numerical ocean models
- Argo
- Gliders
- CTD
- BGC
- NetCDF/scientific formats

## Dimensions
longitude × latitude × depth × time

## Variables
temperature, salinity, currents, chlorophyll and future variables.

## Baseline
MODEL + OBSERVATION + 3D RENDERING

## Research extension
MODEL + OBSERVATION
→ spatiotemporal co-location
→ residual/error field
→ uncertainty
→ feature extraction
→ temporal tracking
→ contextual interaction

## Questions
- Where does reality differ from the model?
- What changed?
- What is uncertain?
- What structure is emerging?
- What should the user investigate next?

# Cross-Disciplinary Design Inspiration

## Awwwards
https://www.awwwards.com/
Study immersive 3D, WebGL, camera choreography, transitions, typography and unusual navigation.

Borrow principles, not decoration.

## Godly
https://godly.website/
Study modern interaction, motion, 3D, typography and experimental UI composition.

## NASA Eyes
https://science.nasa.gov/eyes/
Study how real scientific data is turned into browser-based interactive 3D experiences.

## Mapbox
https://docs.mapbox.com/mapbox-gl-js/
Study globe, camera, 3D terrain and fly-to/path transitions.

## The Pudding
https://pudding.cool/
Study scrollytelling, annotations, narrative sequencing and motion that explains data.

## Games / simulations
Study contextual controls, object-based interaction, state communication and spatial memory.

## Principle
Do not make a scientific dashboard look like an award-winning marketing site. Borrow:
- spatial storytelling
- progressive disclosure
- meaningful motion
- strong hierarchy
- direct manipulation
- contextual UI
